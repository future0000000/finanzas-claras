How to Save Money Fast

Proven strategies to save money fast without sacrificing your quality of life, applicable from your very first month.

Saving fast doesn't mean living on rice and beans for months; it means identifying the biggest money leaks first, before cutting the small expenses that barely move the needle.

1. Automate savings the same day you get paid

Set up an automatic transfer to a separate account as soon as you receive your income. What you don't see in your main account is much harder to spend.

2. Cancel subscriptions you don't use

Review your last 3 months of statements and cancel any subscription you haven't actively used; on average, people pay for 2 or 3 streaming services or apps they barely touch.

3. Apply the 24-hour rule

Before any unplanned purchase above a certain amount, wait 24 hours. Much of the buying impulse disappears with a little time.

4. Negotiate your fixed bills

Internet, car insurance, and phone plans almost always have room for negotiation, especially if you've been a customer for a while and are willing (politely) to threaten switching providers.

5. Cook at home with a weekly menu

Planning your meals for the week drastically reduces both restaurant spending and food waste from groceries that never got used.

6. Sell what you don't use

Clothes, electronics, and furniture you haven't used in over a year can become immediate cash through secondhand selling platforms.
