Types of Insurance: Which Ones You Need

A clear overview of the main types of personal insurance and how to decide which ones are a priority for your situation.

Insurance exists to transfer the financial risk of costly, unexpected events to an insurer, in exchange for a periodic payment called a premium.

Health insurance

Fully or partially covers medical costs from illness or accidents. It's generally considered a high priority, since major medical expenses can seriously jeopardize a family's finances without coverage.

Life insurance

Provides a payout to beneficiaries in the event of the insured's death. It's especially relevant for those with financial dependents (children, a partner, parents) who rely on their income.

Auto insurance

In many countries it's legally required, at least in its liability form, which covers damage to third parties. Broader coverage also protects the vehicle itself against theft or accidents.

Homeowners or renters insurance

Protects the home and its contents against risks like fire, theft, or natural disasters, depending on the policy.

How to prioritize which to get first

A common way to prioritize is evaluating which event would have the most devastating financial impact if it happened without coverage: health and life insurance typically top that list for most people with financial dependents.
