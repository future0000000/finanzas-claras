How to Get Out of Debt

Compare the two most effective methods for getting out of debt and choose which one fits your situation best.

Getting out of debt requires a clear strategy, not just good intentions. The two most studied and widely used methods are the debt snowball and the debt avalanche.

The snowball method

This means paying off the smallest debt first, regardless of its interest rate, while making minimum payments on the rest. Once it's paid off, that payment amount rolls into the next-smallest debt. Its advantage is psychological: it generates quick wins that keep you motivated.

The avalanche method

Here you pay off the debt with the highest interest rate first, regardless of the balance. Mathematically it's more efficient because it reduces the total interest paid over time, though the visible results take longer to appear.

How to choose between the two

If you need constant motivation to stick with the plan, the snowball method usually works better. If you have discipline and prioritize saving on interest, the avalanche is the cheaper option.

Extra steps that speed things up

Freezing credit card use while paying off debt, negotiating lower rates with the bank, and putting any extra income (bonuses, sales, refunds) directly toward debt are strategies that significantly speed up the process.
