Personal Finance for Freelancers

How to organize your personal finances when you have variable income as a freelancer or self-employed worker.

Working independently brings freedom, but also variable income that requires a different financial approach than a fixed salary.

Pay yourself a fixed salary, even if your income varies

A common strategy is to have two accounts: one where all business income comes in, and another where you "pay yourself" a fixed monthly salary, based on the average of previous months, to keep your personal spending stable.

Build a bigger financial cushion

Because of income variability, self-employed workers are generally advised to keep a larger emergency fund than someone with a fixed salary, closer to 6-9 months of essential expenses.

Set aside money for taxes as soon as you invoice

Unlike an employee with automatic withholding, a self-employed person is usually responsible for calculating and setting aside their own taxes. Reserving a percentage of every payment received avoids surprises at filing time.

Diversify your income sources

Relying on a single client or project increases financial risk. Diversifying across several clients or income streams reduces the impact if one of them is lost.

Plan your own retirement and benefits

Without an employer automatically contributing to a pension plan, self-employed workers need to plan and contribute on their own to retirement vehicles and consider private health insurance.
