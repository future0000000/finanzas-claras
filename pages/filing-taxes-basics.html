How to File Taxes: Basic Concepts

Essential concepts to understand how personal income tax filing works, explained in plain language.

Filing taxes sounds complicated, but understanding a few basic concepts helps take the fear out of the process and avoid common mistakes.

Gross income vs taxable income

Gross income is the total you receive before any deductions. Taxable income is the figure your tax is actually calculated on, after applying deductions and exemptions allowed by law.

What deductions are

These are specific expenses the law allows you to subtract from your income before calculating tax, such as certain medical, educational, or housing expenses, depending on the country. They reduce taxable income, not the tax itself directly.

Withholding at the source

Many employers withhold a portion of tax directly from your paycheck throughout the year. When filing, what was withheld is compared against what's actually owed: this can result in a refund or an additional payment.

Keep your receipts throughout the year

Organizing receipts for deductible expenses as they happen, instead of searching for them all at year end, saves time and reduces filing errors.

When it's worth hiring a tax advisor

If you have income from multiple sources, are self-employed, or have complex investments, a certified tax advisor can help you file correctly and claim deductions that are easy to overlook.
